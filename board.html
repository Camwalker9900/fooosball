How these numbers are made

These are not Sleeper's numbers — Sleeper doesn't publish championship odds. They come from simulating the rest of your season 25,000 times and counting how often each team ends up holding the trophy.

Everything describing this season comes from this season: projections, rosters, injuries, the real schedule and the real bye weeks. Everything describing the scoring environment — the league's weekly average, how volatile scoring is, how far apart teams genuinely are — is measured from this league's own history rather than assumed.

  • Team strengthEvery rostered player's Sleeper season projection, scored with your league's own scoring settings rather than generic PPR, then the best legal lineup each week.
  • RandomnessWeekly scores drawn around that mean with a standard deviation of 22.5 points, measured from your league's own previous season rather than assumed, and rescaled if this season is scoring higher or lower.
  • Bye weeksEach week's lineup is rebuilt with players whose NFL team is on bye removed. Only owned players are counted. Unfilled slots score zero; future acquisitions are not assumed.
  • Kickers and defencesSleeper's projections for these two positions are missing whole scoring categories — no points-allowed tiers, no field goals under 40 yards — so both are recalibrated against what they actually scored in your league last season.
  • ScheduleThe actual head-to-head schedule Sleeper has already published for weeks 1–14, not a generic round robin.
  • PlayoffsTop 8 by record then points for, seeded into the real three-round bracket starting week 15.
  • InjuriesPlayers Sleeper lists as out, IR or PUP are dropped from a team's expected lineup, so the slot falls to the next body on the roster. Questionable and doubtful still count in full.
  • Live weeksA week only counts as a result once every NFL game in it is final. Mid-week, scores are banked and only remaining production is simulated. Missing game clocks require an explicitly approximate live estimate.
  • Injury luckAvailability rates and return times are heuristic assumptions. All rostered players are simulated jointly, and legal lineups are rebuilt after absences, including simultaneous injuries and recovery into the playoffs.
  • Breakouts and bustsEach player's true level is drawn fresh every simulated season, with more uncertainty for younger players — so an unproven second-year receiver genuinely goes off in some seasons and disappears in others.
  • What it can't knowThe model does not assume future transactions or value picks, keeper rights, or FAAB. Simulation precision does not establish forecast accuracy.
  • As results landFinal games become fixed results. Player usage updates inform projections; historical team averages and hindsight lineup efficiency are not added as separate strength adjustments.
